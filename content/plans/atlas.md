## Documentation your users will love

Atlas gives your product a thoughtful home for guides, tutorials, and references. A clear sidebar, readable typography, and automatic table of contents help readers find what they need.

## Made to be yours

Edit your content in Markdown, customize the design with Tailwind, and deploy to your preferred static hosting provider. Everything is in your repository, with no proprietary platform in the middle.

## Performance by design

Static HTML, small stylesheets, and lightweight JavaScript create a strong starting point for Lighthouse scores of 95 or higher. Final scores depend on your content, third-party scripts, and hosting.
